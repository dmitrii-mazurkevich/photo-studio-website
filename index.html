About the studio

A shoot you don't have to perform at

Most people say they're not photogenic. Usually that means they were photographed quickly, in bad light, by someone telling them which way to turn their head. We work the other way round: the conversation first, the camera second.

A daylight studio, a full set of strobes for the harder jobs, and location work when the place matters to the story. Retouching is done in-house, not sent out.

How it works

From your first message to finished photographs

A conversation

Twenty minutes: what the photographs are for, where they'll be taken and what they need to do. The idea often changes at this point.

Preparation

Date, location, what to wear, what to bring. For weddings, a written timeline so the day doesn't slip.

The shoot

Unhurried. We show you frames as we go, so you can see what's working instead of wondering.

Selection and retouching

You choose from a private gallery, we retouch what you chose. The unedited files are yours either way.
